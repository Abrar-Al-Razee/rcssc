About RCSSC Portal

Rajshahi Collegiate School Math & Science Club is dedicated to building Bangladesh's next generation of researchers, engineers, and scientific leaders.

Legacy (Est. 1828)

Founded within the historic campus of Rajshahi Collegiate School—the oldest school in Bangladesh—RCSSC carries forward a nearly two-century tradition of academic excellence and intellectual curiosity.

Our Mission

To provide students with a collaborative environment for hands-on experimentation, competitive Olympiad training, robotics workshops, and math problem-solving sessions.
