About RCSSC

Legacy of Scientific Excellence

Rajshahi Collegiate School, established in 1828, holds a prestigious place in the history of education in Bangladesh. The **Rajshahi Collegiate School Math & Science Club (RCSSC)** carries this historic legacy forward by inspiring students to explore mathematics, natural sciences, technology, and analytical problem solving.

Our Mission

To nurture curiosity, foster logical reasoning, and prepare students for national and international STEM competitions.

Core Divisions

Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Astronomy, Informatics, and Robotics research groups.

Achievements

Consistent top performers in National Science Olympiads, Math Olympiads, and inter-school quiz events.
